# Tour

## The sidebar

| View                                    | Shows                                                            |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Today**                               | Tasks that start today or earlier and are still open.            |
| **Upcoming**                            | A week strip, then every coming day, each with its own add.      |
| **Due dates**                           | Tasks with a deadline, by when; select many to move their dates. |
| **Mess Drawer**                         | Tasks not yet put in a list: the place quick ideas land.         |
| **All**                                 | Every open task.                                                 |
| **Calendar**                            | Month, 1 to 14 days, and a year at a glance.                     |
| **Assigned to me** / **Shared with me** | Tasks others gave you, and lists shared with you.                |
| **Closed**                              | Done and won't-do tasks.                                         |
| **Trash**                               | Deleted tasks and lists, restorable for 30 days unless you change it. |
| **Search**                              | Finds tasks by their words, ranked best first (see below).       |
| **Lists**, **Tags**, **Filters**        | Your own lists, nested tags, and saved filters.                  |

Views show how many open tasks they hold, as lists do, and Closed how many closed tasks it lists
(Calendar, Trash and Search show none); so do lists and tags pinned among them. The Today icon shows today's date, and turns over at midnight.

**Edit sidebar** pins, hides and moves views, Search included. While editing, drag a row by its
grip on the left; a swipe anywhere else scrolls. The menu button at the top left folds the sidebar away
and back (each device remembers it); folded away on a desktop, rest the mouse at the window's
left edge and it slides out over the page, going again once the mouse moves off it. At the bottom, a small month shows a dot on days with open
tasks; click a day to open that day's tasks as a list, like Today but for any day (today itself
opens Today). The day page adds new tasks to that day, shows what got done that day, and its arrows
step a day. The arrow at the top right of the small month folds it down to just one week (and
back), and each device remembers which. Its title picks a month, and clicked again a year, to jump
far; the dot between its arrows goes back to now. Under it is the app's version, which links to what
changed. On a phone the main views sit in the bottom bar, and **More** holds the rest of the
sidebar: the views, saved filters, lists and tags. Its pencil (or holding a row) edits it just
like **Edit sidebar** on a computer, and the changes apply on every device. While editing, each
row has a phone button that puts it on the bottom bar or takes it off: up to four views, saved
filters, lists, tags or Search. A row on the bar shows its tab number; tabs go on in the order you
add them, with More always last. With four on the bar, the button says the bar is full.

Dates leave the year off when it is this year: "Thu, Oct 22", "October", but "Jan 4, 2027".

### Upcoming and Due dates

A task that spans days (a start and an end) is not overdue while it runs: from its start until its
end it shows under today, and only once its end has passed does it count as overdue.
Its row shows both dates, start then end (on a phone, the end under the start).

**Today** says all pecked out once nothing is left open or overdue, with what you finished today
still listed under it. **Postpone** on the Overdue group moves its tasks to today, times kept. A
task that repeats weekly on certain days (every Tuesday, say) whose next date is less than a week
away goes there instead, so it never lands on a day it does not repeat on. Monthly and yearly ones
always come to today, so a missed one stays in front of you; so does one that repeats every so
many days, weeks or months with no day of its own, which counts on from there. The undo message
says where each went.

**Upcoming** lists today and every day after it, empty days included, under a strip of the week's
days. More days load as you near the end, and the strip follows the day at the top of the list. On a
wide screen the add bar sticks above the strip and adds to the day picked there.
Swipe the strip sideways (or drag it with the mouse, or use the arrows) to move a week, and the list
goes to the same weekday there; tap a day to jump to it. Going back before today, or picking an earlier day from the
month name above the strip, starts the list on that day; **Today** brings it back, and Upcoming
always opens on today. Each day's **+** adds a task to that day. Overdue tasks sit on top while
the list starts today. Grouping it any other way than by date shows the next 7 days' tasks in those
groups instead.

**Due dates** shows every open task with a due date: Overdue, Due today, Due tomorrow, Due in the
next 7 days and Due later. Each row shows its due date (even when the task also has a start) and
changes it in place. **Select** (or hold a row on a phone), pick tasks or a whole group with
**Select all**, then move them all to **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next week** or a date you pick, or
clear them. Each task keeps its own time of day, and **Undo** puts them back.

**Filter** (the funnel at the top of Today, Upcoming and the Calendar) shows only some lists, tags
and saved filters, in any mix: a task shows when it is in one of the lists picked, has one of the
tags picked (or a tag under one) and matches one of the saved filters picked; a kind with nothing
picked lets everything through. Each of the three keeps its own filter on this device, and the
funnel is colored while one is on. **Clear** shows everything again.

## Tasks

A task has a title, notes (rich text, with `- [ ] ` checklists), subtasks, tags, a priority, a
**start** (a date, a time, or a time span) and an optional hard **due** date. Views place tasks by
their start; the due date is the deadline.

- **Add**: the field at the top of a list, or press <kbd>Q</kbd> anywhere. Type it all in one line:
  see [quick add](quick-add-and-shortcuts.md).
- **Open**: click a task for its details panel; it can be widened or made full screen. The
  expand button at the end of the add field opens a new task straight in the full view, filled
  with what you typed. The **+** button at the bottom right (every screen size) opens that full
  view too, with the title ready to type into; on a phone it fills the screen, and closing it
  closes the task. At the bottom: the
  comments, the task's history and when it was made and last changed. On a phone those wait
  behind a bar at the bottom: tap it, or pull up on it or past the end of the task, and a panel
  as tall as the task's comes up with your finger with the comments and, below them, the history.
  Pull it about a third of the way, or flick it, and it stays; otherwise it slides back. When the task
  was made shows at the left and when it last changed at the right. Panels that slide up close with a swipe down or a tap outside them. Phone rows put
  the title on its own line, with the list and tags under it and the date at the right.
- **Reminders**: in a task's details, **Reminders** opens a list like TickTick's: tick any of
  **On time**, 5 or 30 minutes, an hour or a day early (an all-day task: **On the day**, 1, 2 or 3
  days or a week early, each at 9:00 or the time set in **Settings > Date and time > All-day reminder
  time**), or **None**. **Custom** makes your own: the **Day** or **Week** tab picks how many days
  or weeks early and the wheels the time of day (60 days early at 4:00 PM, say); a timed task also
  has **Minute** and **Hour** tabs for minutes or hours early. It shows when the reminder will come, or that the time has passed. **Constant reminder** keeps a reminder
  on screen until the task is done: on the Android app it keeps sounding and buzzing until you
  open it, dismiss it or press a button, going quiet after 30 minutes; in a browser it comes back
  every 5 minutes for 30 minutes. Its
  **Dismiss** stops that reminder (the task's other reminders still come) and **Snooze 10 min**
  brings it back ten minutes on, for you alone: others on the task are not reminded and the task
  does not change (without a server, it adds a one-time reminder to the task). Nothing
  changes until **OK**. The first reminder asks your browser for permission to show notifications.
  Reminders go to the people the task is assigned to, or to whoever made it when nobody is.
  They appear while PeckList is open or in the background (offline too), and with a server and
  **Settings > Notifications > Notify me when PeckList is closed** turned on for the device, also
  with PeckList closed (on iPhone and iPad, from PeckList added to the Home Screen). A click
  opens the task. A small bell on the row shows a task has reminders. **Settings > Tasks > New tasks >
  Reminder** opens the same list a new task shows (the all-day one at the all-day reminder time,
  or the timed one when new tasks start with a time) to give every new task reminders once it has
  a date.
- **Repeat**: in a task's details, **Repeat** offers daily, weekly on the task's weekday, weekdays,
  monthly on its day and yearly on its date, and **Custom**: every so many days, weeks (on the
  days you pick), months (on its day, on its weekday such as the second Tuesday, or on the last
  day) or years, ending never, on a date or after a number of times, and counted from the task's
  date or from when you finish it. Completing a repeating task, or marking it won't do, keeps a
  closed copy in its history and moves the task to its next date, keeping its time and reopening
  its subtasks. A task without a date starts today. A small arrows icon on the row shows it repeats.
  Quick add reads `every ...` too.
- **A repeating task's dates to come**: the calendar, **Upcoming**, **Next 7 days** and a picked day
  also show where a repeating task falls next, lighter, with a dashed box and the repeat arrows
  (not for one that counts from when you finish it). Its box and its swipe work on that date:
  completing it (or won't do) closes just that date, and the series goes on without it; Move and
  Pin are the whole series'. Opening one shows the task on that date. Deleting a repeating task, or changing its date, asks first: **Just** that date (the
  series goes on without it; a date moved on its own becomes a task of its own), or **Every
  date**. **Skip** under Repeat leaves the date shown out in one tap. Anything else in the details
  changes every date.
- **Notes**: always ready to type in, with no edit mode. Checklist boxes tick with one tap, without
  starting to edit. The small button in their bottom right opens the full editor, with a
  formatting toolbar. Type `/` for blocks (headings, lists, a checklist, a quote, code). Two of
  them change the whole note: **Every line a checkbox** makes a checklist out of what is there,
  one box per line, and **Checkboxes back to lines** undoes that; the full editor has the same as
  one toolbar button. Each heading has a handle at its right: drag it to move the heading with
  everything under it. It goes before any heading of its size or bigger, or to the end, so two
  top-level sections swap with their subsections, and a subsection moves within its section or
  into another.
- **Assigned**: a task you add to a shared list starts assigned to you.
- **Files**: the paperclip at the top of a task's details attaches files, or drop them on the
  details. Pictures show as thumbnails and open large; other files download. Everyone who sees the
  task sees its files; whoever added one, or the list's owner, removes it. Files sync through the
  server (up to `ATTACHMENT_MAX_MB`, 25 MB by default); a larger one is refused before it is added.
- **Complete**: the circle on the left, or <kbd>Space</kbd>. **Won't do** is in the task's menu.
- **Move**: drag it up or down (on a phone, hold it a moment first); the task rides along under
  your finger, and near the top or bottom of the screen the page scrolls on. In a list's own order a
  line shows where it will land: drag it right to make it a subtask, left to undo that, or drop it
  on the middle of another task to nest it there; a list sorted another way switches to its own
  order when you drop. Into another group, the whole group lights up and the task takes its value:
  a day (Today, Tomorrow, a day in Upcoming) or **No date** sets its date (keeping its time),
  **Next 7 days** and **Later** date it to their first day, priority, tag and list groups set its
  priority, swap its tag or move it to that list, and **Pinned** pins it (dragging a task out of
  Pinned unpins it). Overdue only drags out: letting go over it, or over the task's own group,
  changes nothing. Drop it on a list in the sidebar to move it there. Or use **Move to list...** in
  its menu, or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Up</kbd> / <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Down</kbd> / <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Right</kbd> / <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Left</kbd>.
- **Groups**: a group's whole heading row (apart from its buttons) folds and unfolds it.
- **Check off**: ticking a task's box sends the bird. On **Today** it pops out of the box and
  carries the task to the **Closed** count; everywhere else it flies along the row, striking the
  title through. It takes about half a second. With reduced motion on, the task closes at once.
- **Pin**: **Pin to the top** in a task's menu (its **...** in the details) puts it in a **Pinned**
  group at the top of every view it shows in, and it stays in its own group too. Completing it
  lets go of the pin.
- **Swipe** (phones and tablets, like TickTick): swipe a task right to complete it (or reopen a
  closed one); a short swipe left shows **Date**, **Move** and **Delete**, and a full one opens
  the task's date. **Settings > Tasks > Swipe actions** shows each side on a sample task and changes its
  buttons (up to three: Complete, Date, Move, Pin, Won't do or Delete; move them left
  or right, take one away, add another) and what a full swipe does, or turns the full swipe off;
  it is kept per device. Each change can be undone. Swiping up and down still scrolls. Holding a
  task drags it; holding its checkbox opens its menu.
- **Undo**: every change, in the calendar too, shows a small round **Undo** button for a few
  seconds (a ring runs down the time; it sits bottom left on a phone, above the **+** on a big
  screen). Touching it, or nudging it a little, starts the time over; swipe it left or right to
  send it away.
- **Comment**: at the bottom of a task's details. Comments take a little Markdown (`**bold**`,
  `*italic*`, `` `code` ``, `[links](https://...)`) and **@name** for the people on the task (type
  `@` for suggestions). <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> posts. Everyone who sees the task sees its comments; people
  who may change the task may comment (a list's viewers only read). You edit or delete your own;
  a list's owner may delete any on it.
- **History**: under the comments, a timeline of who changed what and when, newest first (Sam
  set the date to Fri, Oct 30), each change marked by an icon for what changed, from when the task was made: its title, dates, priority, list, people, tags,
  notes, repeat, reminders, progress and pin, and completing, deleting or restoring it. A
  person's quick changes to the same thing within ten minutes show as one. The server keeps it
  for a year, so it shows with a server (and while it can be reached).

## Lists

Lists hold tasks. The three dots next to a list's name open its view menu: **Select**, the view
(**List** or **Timeline**), grouping (by date, priority or tag, or **None** for the list's own
order, the one you drag tasks in) and sorting, and, for its owner, editing, archiving or deleting
the list. The built-in views (Today, Upcoming, All, Assigned to me, Shared with me) and every tag
and saved filter have the same menu, plus **List** to group by list. Picking the chosen grouping or
sort again turns it around (an arrow shows which way it runs): Z to A, latest date first (undated
tasks stay last), low priority first, oldest first, or the groups in reverse. Each list and view
keeps its own choice on this device, and the dots turn colored while it differs from the default;
**Restore defaults** at the bottom of the menu goes back.

**Select** picks many tasks at once: each task's box becomes a circle to tap, and each group gets
a circle that picks everything in it. The bar at the bottom (in place of the tabs on a phone) then
completes, dates, moves, tags, prioritizes, pins or deletes them all together; one **Undo** puts
them back, and the cross stops selecting. **Select all** in the bar picks every task shown, then
turns into **Deselect all**.

**Timeline** shows a list's dated tasks as bars across their days, with today marked. Drag a bar
to move the task to other days (its times and length stay), or drag either end to change when it
starts or ends; a click opens it, and **Undo** takes a move back. On a phone a swipe scrolls the
timeline, bars and all; hold a bar still for a moment to pick it up, as on the calendar.
**One per task** gives each task its own row, the one due first at the top and the last at the
bottom; **Fit together** lets tasks that do not overlap share a row, to see more at once. The
magnifiers make days wider or narrower, from months at a glance to roomy days. Tasks without a date wait under the timeline. Each list remembers whether it was last
shown as a List or a Timeline, on this device.

Folding a group (click its heading) folds it everywhere it shows: fold **Overdue** in Today and it
is folded in Upcoming, All and your lists too, until you open it again. Each device remembers.

A list is marked in the sidebar by its color as a dot until you give it an icon: **Edit list**
(or the new-list dialog) offers about 260 line icons drawn in the list's color and about 370
emoji. Each set starts with a row of common ones, and the rest sit in rows that swipe or scroll
sideways; the search above finds either by name ("garden", "car", "money"), and any other emoji
can be typed into it and used. Colors for lists and tags open on your theme's own nine, with the families (Pastel,
Muted, Earthy, Classic, Vivid) beside them, **More themes** for any other theme's set, and the
rainbow circle for a custom color. A list shared with a group shows the group after its name
("Groceries - Household") for everyone, so it never looks like someone's own list of the same name.
Lists can be shared: see [sharing](sharing.md).

Lists used to have sections. They are gone: a list that had them keeps its tasks in the order they
showed in (tasks outside a section first), and the section names go.

## Tags

Tags nest with `/`: `home/garden`. A task tagged `home/garden` also shows under `home`. Pick colors
in a tag's menu; subtasks inherit their parent's tags.

## Calendar

Three views, switched at the top right:

- **Year**: twelve small months, each day shaded by how many open tasks fall on it, from free to
  busy, so free weeks and crowded days stand out. The shades are relative to that year, and the
  exact count is in the day's tooltip and screen reader label. Click a day to open it in Days, or
  a month's name to open that month. It scrolls on from one year into the next and back, with each year's heading between them and the title following the year in view. The arrows go to the start of the next or previous year. Year is for looking ahead,
  so tasks don't drag here and Unscheduled steps aside until you switch back.
- **Month**: the weeks run on without breaks, so the next weeks are always in sight at the end of
  a month. It scrolls freely, a flick carrying it on, and settles with a week at the top (with a
  mouse; on a phone it stays wherever the flick leaves it). Done tasks stay off the calendar. Each day
  lists its tasks as short lines (time first for timed ones, an hourglass for a due date), then
  **+N more**. Pick 1 to 12 weeks on screen with the round number button: four at first, then
  whatever you picked last.
  Click a day (or its **+N more**) for everything on it and a field to add to it. Drag across
  several days, or swipe across them on a phone, to **Show these days** or make a **New task over
  these days**.
- **Days**: 1 to 14 days side by side on a time grid, with an all-day row on top. The round
  number button by the view switch picks how many: press and hold it, run your finger round the
  dial that opens (the number under it rings and grows, and the days follow as you go), and let
  go to keep it. A tap opens the dial to tap a number; with the keyboard, the arrow keys step it. Pinch, or hold Ctrl and use the wheel, to make hours taller or
  shorter. Click an empty time to add a task there. On a phone, swipe sideways to page.

Drag a task to move it to another day or time: with a mouse, just drag; with a finger, hold the
task a moment until it lifts, then drag, so a swipe anywhere (tasks included) only scrolls. Each
move can be undone. In Days, pull the grip at a task's top or bottom edge to change when it
starts or ends; on a phone, hold a task still for a moment to get big grips above and below it
(easy to hit on a short task), and tap elsewhere when done. While you drag or stretch a task,
a label above it shows its start (and end) time, and a dashed outline shows where it will land.
Phones show no scrollbars in the calendar: swipe to scroll. Drag tasks from **Unscheduled** (on a
phone, the calendar-with-a-cross button at the top) onto a day (all day) or a time. A task dropped
on a month day keeps its time. Each device remembers the view, days, weeks and hour height.

## Your profile menu

Your profile picture in the top right (your photo or initials, with a small icon of the sign-in
provider when you signed in through one) opens a menu with **Notifications**,
**Settings**, **Admin** (admins only), **Stats** (your own numbers, as the server counts them),
**Friends and groups**, **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Change password** and **Log out**.

**Settings > Account > Profile** holds your photo: choose a picture, then drag it (or use the arrow keys) and pinch, scroll or use
the slider to zoom until it sits in the circle, and **Save**. It is kept small on your profile, so
friends and people you share with see it too. **Remove photo** goes back to your initials.
Signed in through an identity provider with no photo of your own (never set, or removed): at
sign-in PeckList uses the provider's picture, if it gives one. A photo you set stays yours.

At the bottom of the menu is the version of the page you are using, with a green check when it is
the version your server runs, or a yellow **!** when it is not (a page from before an update):
hover or click it to read why, and **Reload** to get the matching one. In the Android app the
app's own version shows under it, with a check when the app has everything the server's web app
uses, or a **!** when it is too old for some of it (update the app).

**Update available**: when a newer PeckList release is out than your server runs, or than your
Android app, a box at the bottom of the sidebar (at the end of **More** on a phone) says so, with
**Release page** for its notes and downloads and **Skip this release** to hide it until the next
one. The server looks twice a day ([`UPDATE_CHECK`](../configuration/reference.md#update_check)).

**Change password** asks for the new password twice. Every password field has an eye at its right
end that shows what you typed.

**Forgot your password?** shows under the sign-in form when the server sends email
([email](../guides/email.md)). Type your username or email and a link to set a new one arrives,
working once for 30 minutes; setting it signs you in and ends your other sessions. The link only
goes to a **confirmed** email: add yours under **Settings > Account > Email**, then open the link it sends.
An account from an identity provider that marks its email as verified (Google and most
others do) has it confirmed already.

**Notifications** lists reminders, @mentions, comments, tasks someone assigned you or a group
you are in, dibs called on a group's task, and lists someone shared with you, newest first; a red
dot on your picture means some are new. **Settings > Notifications > Tell me about** picks which of those kinds
reach you as a notification on any of your devices (reminders; assignments and dibs; shared lists;
comments and mentions) and whether they vibrate; the page lists everything either way. The Android
app also lists the same kinds under the phone's own notification settings for PeckList, where each
can be silenced or muted the Android way. A new or changed comment tells everyone else who can see
the task (changes to the task itself tell nobody). A click opens the task or list, the cross clears
one and **Clear all** clears the rest. A comment has **Reply** on the left, which opens the task
with its comments shown and scrolled to it, and a thumbs up on the right, which answers with 👍. A
task for a group has **Open task** on the left and **Call dibs** on the right; a task assigned to
you or taken by dibs has **Open task**, a shared list **Open list**. The same buttons come on the
notifications your phone or browser shows, and those show while the app is open too, as soon as
the next sync brings the news (pull down to sync now). Opening the page marks them read. The
server keeps them for 90 days; reminders that fired on this device stay on it.

## Search

**Search** ranks what it finds the way a search engine does (BM25, as Obsidian's Omnisearch): a
word in a task's title counts most, then its tags, its list's name, its parent task, its notes,
its comments and its files' names, and a title that starts with what you typed comes first. Every
word must match somewhere; words match as you type them ("gard" finds "garden"), and a slip in a
longer word is forgiven. A word found only inside another ("paint" in "Repaint") still shows,
after the rest. `"quotes"` keep a phrase together, `-word` leaves out tasks with that word, and
`#tag` asks for tasks with that tag. Open tasks come first, closed ones under **Closed**.

## Settings

**Settings** (the profile menu, or <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd>) goes by subject. On a wide screen the
subjects stand in a list beside the one that is open; on a phone each opens as a page of its own.

- **Account** (with a server): your photo, email, sign-in methods and linked accounts, passkeys and
  API keys; in the Android app, the server too.
- **Appearance**: light, dark or system theme, and colors.
- **Date and time**: your time zone, how dates show, 12 or 24-hour clock, the first day of the week,
  when a due date counts as soon, how long a task with only a start time shows on the calendar, and
  when all-day reminders come.
- **Tasks**: **Lists and tags**, the Closed group, what a new task starts with, and swipe actions.
- **Notifications** (with a server): which kinds reach you, and this device.
- **Your data**: [download or import a copy](apps-and-sync.md#your-data-as-a-file),
  [move from or to another app](apps-and-sync.md#moving-from-or-to-another-app) (TickTick, Todoist,
  Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do, iCalendar and Things), your usage stats choice, and start over.
- **Support PeckList**: a thank you, and a link to give a tip.

**Passkeys** (on a server with accounts, on an HTTPS address with a name, not an IP address) sign you in with your fingerprint,
face or screen lock instead of a password. **Add a passkey** under **Settings > Account > Passkeys**: your
phone, computer or password manager keeps it, synced to your other devices when it is iCloud
Keychain, Google Password Manager, 1Password, Bitwarden and the like. Then **Sign in with a
passkey** on the sign-in page needs no username (the browser may also offer it right in the
username field). Name, rename and remove them there; with one, **Remove password** lets you do
without a password altogether. Removing your last way in is refused. Lost a phone with your only
passkey? An admin removes it and sends you a sign-in link.

**API keys** (on a server with accounts) let a script or another app use PeckList as you:
give each a name, an expiry (or none) and only the permissions it needs (read or change tasks,
download or upload attachments, notifications, and admin for admins), copy it once, and revoke it
whenever you like. Each shows when it was made, when it expires and when it was last used. See
[the HTTP API](../reference/http-api.md#signing-in-from-a-script).

**Show the Closed group** (under Lists and tags) hides or shows the done and won't-do tasks
under lists and views on this device; a long press (or right-click) on a **Closed** group's
header hides it too. The **Closed** view and search always show them.

**Lists and tags** gathers them on one page: drag a list by its grip to set the order the menu
shows them in, and from each row change its name, icon and color (tap it), who it is shared with,
people and groups (the share button), pin it to the top of the menu, archive it or delete it.
Archived lists wait at the end with Unarchive. Tags the same: tap one to rename it, nest it or
change its color, pin it, or delete it.

**Clean up after** (Settings > Your data): how long deleted, completed and won't-do tasks stay, 30 days
unless you change it. Pick 7 or 15 days, or 2 months up to a year. A completed or won't-do task moves to
the Trash after that long, and you can still restore it from there. A task's comments and files go to the
Trash with it and come back with it. What is in the Trash is erased for
good after the same time, and **Empty trash** does it now. Only a list's owner can erase its trash, so
tasks from lists others own sit in their own group, and Empty trash leaves them. Completed and won't-do tasks count from the day they were closed, so old history you import moves to the Trash the next time the app opens: pick a longer time first if you want to keep it.
A task stays while one of its subtasks is open or has time left, and a completed subtask stays with a
task that stays. Picking a shorter time asks first if it would erase anything right away.

**Start over** (last on the page): **Delete all lists, tasks and tags** moves every list, task and tag
of yours to the Trash at once (lists others share with you stay), and Undo brings them back. Tick
**Also empty the Trash** to delete them, and everything already in the Trash, for good.

**Date format**: the language's own (Automatic), a ready-made one such as `2026-10-24` or
`24.10.2026`, or **Custom...** to type a pattern such as `YYYYMMDD` or `ddd D MMM`: `YYYY` 2026,
`YY` 26, `MMMM` October, `MMM` Oct, `MM` 10, `M` 10, `DD` 05, `D` 5, `dddd` Monday, `ddd` Mon,
and `[text]` as is. It shows today's date in the pattern as you type. Today, Tomorrow and
Yesterday keep their names.

**Colors**: pick one of 30 palettes, from calm (PeckList Forest, the default, then Ocean, Sea glass,
Graphite) and soft (Rose, Blossom, Peach, Lavender, Mint) to bright (Electric, Tropical, Citrus, Berry) and deep (Sunset,
Midnight, Coffee), loud (Neon, Arcade, Fiesta, Volt, Rainbow, Lagoon, Sunburst) and pretty
(Bubblegum, Cotton candy, Lilac, Rose gold, Peony, Orchid, Champagne), then change any of its
colors. The loud and pretty ones also wash the page in their color. **Reset colors** goes back to the palette's own colors. Picking
another palette asks whether to recolor your lists and tags too: each moves to the same spot in
the new palette's set (grays, and lists you cannot change, stay as they are).

**Time zone**: "today", due dates, the calendar and reminders follow it. Until you pick one, it is
the server's when its admin set `TZ`, and otherwise the zone of the device you are on, so it follows
you when you travel. Pick one to keep it fixed wherever you are.

Your apps tell the server their device's zone when it changes, so with no zone picked, reminders
from the server and snoozing from a notification follow you too. The server keeps only the latest,
which says roughly where you are. It is not shown in the app, to admins or anyone else, and it is not
in backups or exports; whoever runs the server can still read its database. It goes when your
account does. A reminder whose new time has already passed when you arrive somewhere later in the day
comes at once; one that already went comes again at the new time if the task is still open. If two
of your devices are in different zones, the server follows whichever told it last, so a reminder can
come twice.

**New tasks**: what a new task starts with: a list (for tasks added outside a list), a priority, a
date (none, today, tomorrow or in a week) with an optional time, reminders, the time all-day
reminders come at, and on a server whether tasks you add to shared lists start assigned to you.
Times are shown on your clock (Settings > Date and time > Clock) and set with the same wheels as a task's date. Where you add a task still wins: in a list it goes to
that list, in Today or on a day it gets that date, and what you type in quick add wins over both.
