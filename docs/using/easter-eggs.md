# Easter eggs

PeckList hides fifteen small surprises. They're hidden on purpose, so each trigger and what it does
sits behind a spoiler below: open only the ones you want to know.

- **None of them changes your data.** They are only something to look at, and they go away on
  their own, most after a few seconds. The longest, typewriter mode, lasts two minutes, and the dog
  stays until you tap it.
- **Reduced motion:** if your device asks for reduced motion, a short line at the top of the screen
  says what you found, and nothing moves.
- A few make a short sound, if the browser allows it.
- They work in the web app and the Android app. A few need a keyboard, and a few need a phone.
- A search word has to be the whole search, in upper or lower case.
- No egg sends anything of its own. What the apps count is listed under
  [Privacy and telemetry](privacy.md#what-the-apps-count).

## 1. The party

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** on a keyboard, type <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd>
<kbd>→</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> anywhere except in a text field.
Or search for `party time` or `confetti`.

**What happens:** confetti rains down for about three seconds, and "You found the party!" shows at
the top.

</details>

## 2. The barrel roll

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** search for `do a barrel roll`.

**What happens:** the whole app does a full turn, and says "Wheee!".

</details>

## 3. The piñata

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** complete a task with `piñata` (or `pinata`) anywhere in its title.

**What happens:** candy bursts out for about three seconds: "Piñata smashed! Have some candy."

</details>

## 4. Retro mode

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** search for `insert coin`.

**What happens:** "Player 1 ready." For a minute, every font is an old monospace one, faint scan
lines cover the screen, and each task you complete makes a little console blip.

</details>

## 5. Gravity

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** shake your phone hard, about four times within a second.

**What happens:** the tasks on screen fall off the bottom one after another and bounce back into
place: "Whoa, careful! Everything fell over."

</details>

## 6. The napping dog

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** hold the **+** (add a task) button for 3 seconds.

**What happens:** a dog trots in, sits on the button, curls up and goes to sleep, snoring little z's.
It stays until you tap it, then gets up and trots off the screen. The button doesn't open a new
task after the hold.

</details>

## 7. Fireworks

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** on **Today**, complete its last open task, so nothing open or overdue is left. Or open
Today when it's already finished: nothing open or overdue, and at least one task done today. On
opening, it happens once a day in each browser tab.

**What happens:** fireworks for about four seconds: "Today is done! Fireworks!"

</details>

## 8. The rubber duck

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** post a comment that says just `quack` (a `.` or `!` after it is fine).

**What happens:** a rubber duck turns up in the corner for a few seconds, asking "Quack?": "Quack.
Go on, explain it to me."

</details>

## 9. Today winks

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** tap **Today** (in the sidebar or the bottom bar) 5 times within two and a half seconds.

**What happens:** the date on the Today icon spins round and winks at you: "Today winks back."

</details>

## 10. Disco

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** in **Settings > Appearance**, click a palette 5 times in a row, each click within half
a second of the last. Clicking the same palette counts.

**What happens:** for two seconds the colors jump five times a second and a big DISCO sign flashes.

</details>

## 11. The season

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** on a touch screen, pull down to refresh 3 times within 30 seconds.

**What happens:** "Look, the season!" For about seven seconds, the season drifts down the screen:
snowflakes from December to February, blossoms from March to May, bubbles and sunshine from June
to August, and leaves from September to November.

</details>

## 12. The trophy

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** empty the **Mess Drawer** yourself, by completing or moving its last open task.

**What happens:** a trophy pops up in the middle of the screen for a few seconds: "Mess Drawer
empty!"

</details>

## 13. Follow the white rabbit

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** search for `follow the white rabbit`.

**What happens:** your own task titles rain down the screen like falling code for about seven
seconds: "Wake up. The tasks have you."

</details>

## 14. Typewriter mode

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** add a task titled `clickety clack`, or rename one to it.

**What happens:** "Clickety clack: typewriter mode for two minutes." The task titles on screen type
themselves out one after another, with a blinking caret and the clack of keys, and for two minutes
titles type themselves out as they appear.

</details>

## 15. Pecky

<details>
<summary>Trigger and effect</summary>

**Trigger:** complete 5 tasks within 2 minutes.

**What happens:** Pecky, a little bird, pops up for a few seconds and cheers you on with "Keep
pecking!": "Pecky says: 5 in a row!"

</details>
