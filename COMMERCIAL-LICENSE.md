# Commercial license

Punchlist is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). Anyone may use, host,
modify and share it, including commercially, as long as they pass the same freedoms on: changes
to a version that people use over a network must be published under the same license.

Organizations that cannot or do not want to meet those terms (for example, to run a modified
Punchlist as a service without publishing the changes, or to embed it in a proprietary product)
can buy a commercial license from the copyright holder. It grants the same software under terms
without the copyleft and source-offer obligations, per organization, for a yearly fee that
includes the versions released during the term.

Terms and prices are being written. Until the purchase page exists, open an issue titled
"Commercial license" or write to the maintainer through the GitLab profile.

Personal, family and noncommercial use never needs this: the AGPL already allows it.
