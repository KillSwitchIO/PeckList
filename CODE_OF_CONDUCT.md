# Code of conduct

PeckList wants to be a project anyone can bring a question, a bug or a change to, whatever their
experience, background or identity. This applies in every PeckList space: issues, merge requests,
the wiki, the community chat, and anywhere you speak for the project.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new. Everyone asked a first question once.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Disagree about ideas and code, not about people. Explain why, and accept a "no" gracefully.
- Keep it on topic, and keep other people's private information private.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to anyone affected, and learn from them.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or deliberate intimidation, in public or in private messages.
- Comments that demean people for who they are: their gender, gender identity or expression,
  sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion,
  or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information (an address, an email, a real name they do not use
  here) without their permission.
- Trolling, spam, or repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, open a
[confidential issue](https://gitlab.com/killswitch10/punchlist/-/issues/new?issue[confidential]=true)
(tick **This issue is confidential**) addressed to the maintainers, or message a moderator in the
community chat. Only maintainers can read confidential issues. Reports are read promptly and kept
as private as the situation allows; reporting in good faith never counts against you.

## What happens

Maintainers decide what a breach calls for, from a private word, to a public warning, to removing
comments, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. They explain the decision to
the person involved where that is safe. Maintainers who break this code are held to it the same way.
